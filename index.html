IS THE INITIAL DERMATOLOGIST CONSULTATION INCLUDED?

Yes. Every appointment starts with a thorough visual and digital skin examination with our doctor to tailor the exact Korean protocol for your skin condition.

HOW MANY SESSIONS ARE REQUIRED FOR PIGMENTATION?

Most patients notice significant clearing after 2–4 sessions spaced 3 weeks apart, depending on whether the pigmentation is epidermal or deep dermal melasma.

WHAT IS THE DOWNTIME FOR KOREAN GLASS SKIN PROTOCOLS?

Virtually zero. You can immediately return to work or social events with an instant dewy, hydrated glass skin glow. Mild erythema, if any, settles within 20 minutes.

CAN I BRING PHOTOS OR PREVIOUS PRESCRIPTIONS?

Absolutely. We encourage bringing any past medical reports, products, or reference goals so we can evaluate your skin history holistically.

DO YOU ACCEPT WALK-IN APPOINTMENTS IN INDORE?

To guarantee personalized doctor time and zero waiting, we operate primarily on advance bookings. Walk-ins are accommodated subject to schedule availability.

WHAT PRODUCTS AND SERUMS DO YOU USE EXACTLY?

We use certified Korean medical aesthetic formulations, pharmaceutical-grade hyaluronic acids, glutathione boosters, and FDA-cleared laser systems only.
